Our Story

Building a Real-World Community

We believe friendship is built face to face - over a board game, a shared meal, or a sunset by the sea.

Our Mission

Journeya exists to bring people together through one of the simplest joys in life: playing. In a world that keeps us glued to screens, we create real spaces where you can laugh, compete, share stories and make friends that stick.

Whether it's a casual game night in Cairo or a professional networking event, every Journeya experience is designed around one thing: genuine connection.
